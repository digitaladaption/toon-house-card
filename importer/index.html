Floor plan importer

Turn a floor plan picture into a house for Toon House Card. The picture stays on this computer.

Drop a floor plan here

An estate agent's plan works well: a JPG, PNG or WebP with solid or dark walls. For a PDF, take a screenshot first.

It works best when the front of the house is at the bottom of the picture.

How it goes

  1. Choose your plan. The rooms it finds are tinted.
  2. Set the scale: click a room, type its size as printed on the plan and press Set scale. Or use Measure for scale and drag along a length you know.
  3. Name the rooms. The name decides the colours (kitchen, bathroom, bedroom...).
  4. Fix anything it got wrong with the tools, and click the front door to mark it.
  5. Download house.json.
